# References & Documentation Sources

This platform builds upon official API specifications, international
safety guidelines, municipal cancellation procedures, and risk
assessment frameworks:

## APIs & Technical Specifications

-   [Ticketmaster Discovery API
    Documentation](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/products-and-docs/apis/discovery-api/v2/):
    Endpoint documentation for querying live event metadata, venue
    coordinates, and categories.

-   [Open-Meteo Weather API Docs](https://open-meteo.com/): Technical
    specifications for real-time hourly forecasts, WMO weather codes,
    and atmospheric parameters.

-   [Open-Meteo Historical Forecast
    API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api):
    Reference for querying historical weather reanalysis and archived
    model data for model backtesting.

## Event Weather Safety & Emergency Planning

-   [National Weather Service Event Ready Guide (NWS
    PDF)](https://www.weather.gov/media/crh/eventready/Event_Ready_Guide.pdf):
    Comprehensive weather emergency planning guide published by the NWS
    for event managers.

-   [Event Safety Alliance Weather Emergency Planning
    Guide](https://eu.taf.cz/why-every-live-event-needs-a-weather-emergency-plan):
    Structural wind limits, rigging load tolerances, and stage hold
    protocols for live outdoor productions.

-   [Outdoor Event Weather Safety Guide (HSE
    Blog)](https://www.hseblog.com/outdoor-event-weather-safety/):
    Operational health and safety guide detailing lightning distance
    safety rules (30/30 rule) and extreme temperature protocols.

## European & Municipal Risk Frameworks

-   [YOUROPE --- Introduction to Risk
    Assessment](https://yourope.org/know-how/wtb-13-introduction-risk-assessment/):
    Standardized risk assessment methodologies developed by the European
    Festival Association.

-   [YOUROPE --- Risk Assessment Template: Heavy
    Rain](https://yourope.org/know-how/wtb-17-risk-assessment-template-heavy-rain/):
    Operational matrix for managing heavy rainfall, ground saturation,
    and site evacuation at outdoor festivals.

-   [Oxford City Council Outdoor Event Adverse Weather & Cancellation
    Procedure](https://www.oxford.gov.uk/organising-event-land/adverse-weather-outdoor-event-cancellation-procedure):
    Official municipal guidelines outlining legal and operational steps
    for delaying or canceling outdoor events due to weather.

## Financial Impact & Logistics

-   [The Financial Impact of Weather on Outdoor Events (Visual
    Crossing)](https://www.visualcrossing.com/resources/blog/outdoor-event-planning-for-unpredictable-weather-safer-smarter-venue-and-logistics-management/):
    Industry study analyzing attendance "no-show" drop-off rates and
    food & beverage revenue losses during adverse weather.
